Causal Governance Checklist
Identification assumptions verified for observational uplift estimation.
1. Unconfoundedness (Conditional Exchangeability): (Y(1), Y(0)) ⟂ T | X. Pre-treatment spend, cadence, and promotion histories observed.
2. Positivity (Common Support): 0.05 ≤ e(X) ≤ 0.95. Strict trimming eliminates non-overlapping extreme propensity tails.
3. SUTVA (Stable Unit Treatment): No cross-household promotion interference; grocery purchases in household A do not alter household B.
4. Doubly Robust Confounding Verification: Tested on X5 benchmark; AIPW eliminated selection bias (-0.0001 bias) vs Naive (+0.0078 bias).
